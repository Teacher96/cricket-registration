GROUND RULES (read once, ignore forever)

  1. Be at the ground 10 minutes before the start. Late players bowl the first over.
  2. Everyone bats and everyone bowls (the keeper's gloves are not an excuse).
  3. The umpire is always right. If not, see rule number one.
  4. Whoever hits the ball into a neighbour's house goes to fetch it.
  5. Winners pick the snacks. Losers pay for the chai.
  6. No arguing after 8:00 PM. The ground lights are not ours.
